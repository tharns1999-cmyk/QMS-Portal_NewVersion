# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary audience: General employees and Document Control Center (DCC) staff who create and manage quality documents.
Secondary audience: Managers and Executives who approve workflows and view summary reports.

## Product Purpose
To transform paper-based document management into a 100% digital, fully traceable process. Success looks like a streamlined, compliant QMS ecosystem with zero paper waste and complete auditability at every step.

## Positioning
A comprehensive QMS document control hub that automatically integrates with the organizational structure for seamless workflow routing.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Clean, and Clear. The interface should evoke the modern, flexible feel of tools like Notion or Asana, making enterprise tasks feel lightweight and approachable.

## Anti-references
Absolutely no legacy enterprise UI. Avoid dense, clunky, or overly complex navigation structures that make the system feel outdated or intimidating.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over density**: Space is a premium; let the data breathe so users of all ages can read comfortably.
- **Modern enterprise**: Combine the strict compliance rules of QMS with the lightweight, flexible feel of modern SaaS.
- **Accessible by default**: High contrast and large, readable typography are core to the experience for all employees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast text and UI elements. Large, readable typography designed to be comfortable for employees of all age groups.
